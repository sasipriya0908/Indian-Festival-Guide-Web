ONAM 🌸

Kerala's grand festival of harvest, flowers, and legend

Introduction

Onam is an annual Hindu harvest festival celebrated predominantly in the state of Kerala. It is the official state festival, spanning over ten days with majestic cultural celebrations that represent the spirit, traditions, and rich heritage of the region.

Significance

Onam commemorates the golden reign of King Mahabali, a mythical wise king who ruled Kerala with absolute prosperity, equality, and righteousness. According to legend, his spirit visits Kerala every year during Onam to check on his beloved subjects, making it a festival of joy, gratitude, and welcoming him back.

The Celebration

The festivities are characterized by various spectacular cultural events. Beautiful circular floral arrangements known as "Pookkalam" are laid out in front of homes. The festival also features the legendary Vallam Kali (thrilling snake boat races), Pulikali (vibrant tiger dances), and traditional dances like Kaikottikali.

Traditional Cuisines

The highlight of Onam is the "Onam Sadhya," a spectacular multi-course vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf. It features over 24 to 28 traditional dishes, including Avial, Thoran, Kalan, Olan, Sambar, Rasam, banana chips, and a variety of delicious sweet rice puddings called Payasam.
