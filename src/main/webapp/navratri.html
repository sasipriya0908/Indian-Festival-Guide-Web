NAVRATRI 💃

Honoring the Divine Feminine through dance and prayer

Introduction

Navratri, meaning "nine nights," is a major Hindu festival dedicated to the worship of Goddess Durga. It is a time of intense spiritual devotion, fasting, and vibrant community celebrations across India.

Significance

The festival celebrates the victory of Goddess Durga over the buffalo demon Mahishasura, representing the triumph of good over evil. Each of the nine days is dedicated to a different form of the Goddess (Shakti).

The Celebration

In Gujarat and across many cities, Navratri is famous for Garba and Dandiya Raas—rhythmic folk dances performed in large circles. People dress in exquisite traditional attire like Chaniya Choli and Kedarayu, dancing late into the night.

Traditional Cuisines

Since many observe fasts, special "Vrat" foods like Sabudana Khichdi, Singhare ki Puri, and various milk-based sweets are prepared and shared.
