HOLI 🎨

A vibrant celebration of love, spring, and joy

Introduction

Holi is one of India's most vibrant and joyful festivals. Known as the "Festival of Colors," it marks the arrival of spring and the victory of good over evil. It is a time when people come together to sing, dance, and play with colorful powders.

History & Myth

Holi's origins are rooted in the legend of Prahlad and Holika, signifying the triumph of devotion over ego. It also celebrates the eternal love of Radha and Krishna, who famously played with colors in Vrindavan.

The Celebration

People celebrate by smearing each other with "Gulal" (colored powder) and drenching friends with water. Music, drums, and community dancing are central to the festivities, breaking down social barriers in a sea of color.

Traditional Cuisines

Traditional Holi treats include Gujiya (sweet dumplings), Malpua, Dahi Bhalla, and the refreshing Thandai, often infused with festive spirits.
